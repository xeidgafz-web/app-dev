# X-MEN SERIES

## About the Series
X-Men is a superhero series about mutants with extraordinary powers who protect humanity and fight evil.

## Main Characters
- Wolverine – Has healing abilities and sharp claws.
- Professor X – A powerful telepath and leader.
- Cyclops – Shoots energy beams from his eyes.
- Storm – Controls the weather.
- Jean Grey – Has telepathic and telekinetic powers.
- Beast – Has superhuman strength and intelligence.

## Why I Like This Series
I like X-Men because it has exciting action, amazing superpowers, and meaningful stories. It teaches friendship, teamwork, courage, and acceptance.

## My Favorite Character
Wolverine is my favorite character because he is strong, brave, and never gives up.

## Conclusion
X-Men teaches us that being different is not a weakness but a strength.
